# legal-logic: paired cases

All facts are fictional.

## 1. Independent evidence

Opposing draft (both variants): "The inspector could reasonably infer the seal had opened."

- **A.** The only observation is a warning light that, under the stipulated operating conditions, comes on equally often whether the seal is open or closed.
- **B.** Same warning light. The inspector also saw the seal hanging free.

Expected: In A, the light gives no comparative support for an open seal over a closed one, so the inference fails. In B, the direct observation is an independent route to the same conclusion. A reviewer that repeats the attack on the warning light in B, without addressing the direct observation, has missed the independent evidence.
